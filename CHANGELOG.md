# Changes

## 1.2

- Hearts on the road's shoulder: leave the lane to take one. A heart mends
  the car to half, or adds a quarter, up to 125%.
- Wings, rarely: only on clear summer days, in farmland, desert or jungle.
  The car flies free of the road for a while and glides back down. Police
  below fall far behind and only catch up if you stand still a long time.

## 1.1

- The car wears: every knock costs something, by how hard it was. Below 60% it
  smokes, below 30% it coughs, tops out at 220 and pulls to one side; at zero
  the engine dies. Gas stations repair it, or give you another car.
- Heavy vehicles take less harm: the bus and the bowling ball half as much.
- The pump button is smaller, green, out of the thumb's way, and breathing.

## 1.0

The first release.

- An endless, generated road through seven countries, blending at the borders.
- Traffic both ways, lanes by country, carriageways that part and rejoin.
- Forks with signs; the road you take decides the next country.
- Crossroads with lights and cross traffic; police chases.
- Time of day, seasons, rain, snow and fog; headlights at night.
- Fuel, gas stations with signs, an automatic pull-in, a change of cars.
- Rainbow puddles, black holes, a saucer and a rabbit hole among the stars,
  a wave and a drowned city.
- Rallies against the clock and over a set time, kept and repeatable.
- Sixteen vehicles; synthesized engine, siren, rain and effects.
