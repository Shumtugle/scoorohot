SCOOROHOT'78

A new road every ride.

A retro racing game for Android. The road is built just ahead of you as you drive, so it never ends and is never the same twice.

Everything you see and hear is made by the game itself: the road, the cars, the countryside, the engine note. No images, no recordings.

Put a finger on the screen and the car drives. Slide to steer. Drag down to brake.

On the road

  • Seven countries blending into one another, from the coast to the taiga.
  • Traffic both ways, forks with signs, crossroads with lights.
  • Day and night, seasons, rain, snow and fog.
  • Fuel, gas stations, and a change of cars at the pumps.
  • Police chases. A saucer. Black holes. A wave.
  • Rallies against the clock, kept and repeatable on the same road.
